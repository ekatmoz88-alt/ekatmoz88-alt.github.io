Extracted customer levels are listed without implying a parent-child sequence: Account_det, City_lev_def, State_Province, Country, WAREHOUS / WAREHOUSE and SHIP_TO. Summary levels marked active: CUSTOMER_DIM.SHIP_TO, ACCOUNT.City_lev_def and CUSTOMER_DIM.WAREHOUSE. Account_det, State_Province, Country, Region and Total were not selected in the extracted summary-level settings. The supplied checklist records that summary-level definitions were not included in the AW DDL and are ignored by that script. Parent-child relationships among the first four levels are not confirmed by the available extraction.

Value-based hierarchy keys use Level Key + Parent Key. The extraction also records a ragged / skip-level hierarchy setting in the AW model.
